FIGURE 04

Book exchange with deposits and cash difference

Use extend for the conditional cash difference; the proposer is not assumed to be the payer.

SRS trace: UC-06, UC-07, UC-08, UC-09.

100%Open SVGPNGPlantUML source
Business notes and UML relationships
  • An exchange party is the Initiator or Recipient for a transaction; only the party whose response is pending may accept.
  • A Proposed exchange does not reserve books; acceptance reserves both books atomically or neither.
  • A party’s deposit and cash difference are held atomically in one funding action.
  • Shipping opens only after both deposits and the cash difference are funded; each leg has its own inspection deadline.

FIGURE 05

Wallet top-up and simulated withdrawal

Callbacks and reconciliation are separate interactions. Do not include successful payout as part of approval.

SRS trace: UC-13; part of UC-16.

100%Open SVGPNGPlantUML source
Business notes and UML relationships
  • Withdrawal approval does not mean money has been transferred; Staff cannot approve withdrawals.
  • An unknown Processing result waits for reconciliation; do not refund the wallet and retry simultaneously.
  • Every money movement is balanced and idempotent; there is no direct Edit balance use case.
  • Payout execution starts after approval and is supported by the Payment Simulator.

Disputes decisions appeals and returns

Separate review, decision, appeal and execution; do not use a use-case diagram as an activity diagram.

SRS trace: UC-10, UC-11, UC-12.

100%Open SVGPNGPlantUML source
Business notes and UML relationships
  • A transaction can contain claims from both parties in one dispute; only related funds are held.
  • An appeal is a separate goal, not a time-sequenced extension of Issue decision.
  • The Admin resolving an appeal must be independent from the original decision maker.
  • Execution starts after the decision becomes effective; return and settlement have separate progress.
